# Put AI on the bottleneck: script skeleton

About 8.5 minutes. Fill the [brackets] before filming.

## How to carry it

1. Open on the payoff, then give one line of proof. Keep it to one sentence, because a full bio before the payoff loses viewers.
2. Bring in each credential where it backs a claim: your software and AI work in part 1, your plant years in part 4. Tell a short story there instead of listing titles.
3. Ask the viewer before you answer ("Should I buy it?"), then pause for 1 second.
4. Say the same numbers that are on screen.
5. Give the counterpoint in part 3: the mixer pays off once it's on the bottleneck. That keeps the video from sounding anti-AI.

## Skeleton

| Time | On screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Your face, full frame | Hook: "Someone offers you a $100 machine that saves 18 minutes a day. Do you buy it?" Proof: "I spent [X] years as an operations consultant in US manufacturing plants and [Y] years as a software engineer, the last [Z] building AI for businesses." Promise: "By the end you'll know the one question to ask before you spend money on AI." |
| 0:30 | Board | Roadmap: a lemonade stand, a juice box plant, then your business. |
| 0:45 | Part 1 | Your AI work: "Most AI requests I get look like this." Notes bots, invoice OCR, drafts, inbox sorting. Hours saved, revenue flat. Takeaway: ask what limits your output. |
| 1:45 | Part 2 | The stand: $1 a cup, 20 cups a day, 20 minutes of mixing. The offer, then ask and pause. Mixing drops from 20 minutes to 2. Extra revenue: $0. |
| 3:00 | Part 3 | Capacity 100 cups, sales 20, so 80 sit idle. The bottleneck is customers. Where the 30 minutes should go. The twist: at 100 cups you batch 5 times, and the mixer saves 90 minutes a day. It was a good buy at the wrong time. |
| 4:15 | Part 4 | Plant story: [one real line where a client fixed the wrong machine]. The four machines and their rates. Upgrade the labeler: nothing changes. Upgrade the filler: output up 33%. Name it: Theory of Constraints. |
| 5:45 | Part 5 | The same line in a business. Most businesses are short on customers. Where AI usually goes, then where it pays. [One example from your own client work.] |
| 7:00 | Part 6 | The 4-step checklist. Ask viewers to name their own bottleneck as you go. End on the line on screen. |
| 8:00 | Your face, full frame | One-sentence recap. Ask: "Comment your bottleneck below." Then your call to action. |

## Filming

- Record one row per take. If you trip on a sentence, pause and restart it. The cut is easy in Take.
- Look at the lens for the hook and for each part's last line.
