# Put AI on the bottleneck

The point: most businesses add AI to save time on work that isn't what limits them. AI pays when it goes on the bottleneck, and for most businesses the bottleneck is demand.

Parts (about 50 seconds and 5 steps each):

1. AI for its own sake. AI lands on admin and delivery, saves hours, and revenue stays flat.
2. The $100 mixer. A lemonade stand sells 20 cups a day. A mixer cuts mixing from 20 minutes to 2 and adds $0.
3. The limit is customers. The stand can make 100 cups. At 100 cups it batches 5 times a day, and then the mixer saves 90 minutes.
4. One bottleneck sets the output. On a juice box line, upgrading the labeler changes nothing and upgrading the filler lifts output 33%.
5. Most businesses are demand-constrained. AI pays on content, outreach, follow-up and the sales funnel.
6. Find your bottleneck first. Map the line, find the slowest step, point AI there, and re-check when it moves.
